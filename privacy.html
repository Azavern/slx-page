What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, name, and email address. We use them only to identify your player account in the game.

We also store your in-game progress on our server (level, experience, in-game money, reputation, hardware, missions, and leaderboard data) so you can continue playing on any device.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real IP address for display to other players, your contacts, your location, or any data from other apps on your device. We do not serve ads and we do not sell or share your data with third parties.

How we use your data

  • To create and maintain your player account.
  • To save and restore your game progress.
  • To show your player name, level, and reputation on the in-game leaderboard.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.
